About Charmi

Someone in your corner, with a plan.

Hello, I'm Charmi. I became a psychologist for a plain reason: I wanted to be around at the moment things start to shift for someone. I've spent my career as a mental health counsellor working with children, adolescents, parents, young adults and adults, and that range is my favourite part of the job.

Sessions with me are unhurried and non-judgmental. With adults I lean on CBT, DBT and mindfulness-based approaches. With children it's play and art, because that's how children talk. Either way, the method bends to the person, not the other way around.

What keeps me in this work is watching people surprise themselves. Saying the thing out loud for the first time, or finally sleeping through the night. That part never gets old.

Common questions

Things people ask before booking

Is what I share confidential?
Yes. What you share stays between us, with the standard exceptions required by law and ethics: risk of serious harm to yourself or others, or a court order. We'll go over this in the first session.
Do you see children?
Yes, children and teens are a big part of this practice. Sessions use play and art rather than an hour of sitting and talking, and parents get regular check-ins so everyone knows how it's going.
Is it normal to seek therapy?
Yes, and more normal every year. Most people who come here aren't in crisis; they're people with jobs, families and group chats who want one hour that belongs only to them. If you're asking the question, that's usually reason enough to try a session.
Do you also take group sessions?
Yes. Small groups run from time to time around shared themes like stress, grief and parenting. Community has its own kind of medicine; hearing "me too" from others in the room does something one-to-one sessions can't. Message us to ask what's currently running.
Do I need a diagnosis or a referral?
No. You don't need a referral or a "big enough" problem. If something is taking up space in your head, that's reason enough.
What happens in the first session?
Mostly, you talk and I listen. We map what's going on, what you'd like to be different, and how we might work together. You set the pace. Nothing has to be shared before you're ready.
Online or in-person, does it matter?
Online sessions work well for most people; pick whichever format you'll actually show up to. For online you'll need a quiet, private spot and a stable connection.
How do payments and packages work?
Once you book a slot, we reach out on WhatsApp to confirm it and share payment details, so there is nothing to pay online while booking. For multi-session packages, message us and we will set one up for you.
What if I need to cancel or reschedule?
Reschedule or cancel free up to 12 hours before your session. Later than that, the session fee applies.
How many sessions will I need?
It depends on what we're working on. Some people come for six focused sessions; others stay longer. We review progress openly every few weeks, so you're never left guessing why you're still coming.
